Fistful of Lead

Crew Builder

Builds the standard roster: 1 Leader (free Leader trait + 3 picks, 5 slots) · 1 Specialist (2 traits, 4 slots) · 3 Regular-or-Grunt slots (1 trait, 3 slots — a Grunt group shares 1 trait and 2 slots). Everything is stateless: nothing saves automatically — use Export JSON before you close the tab, and Import JSON to resume later. Crew Traits that restructure the roster (Heroes All, Special Ops, Horde, Above Average, Tactical) are shown as a rules note only — they don't auto-change slot counts yet.
Homebrew tool for Fistful of Lead: Galactic Heroes, 2nd Edition. All Trait/Equipment text is condensed from the rulebook — check the full rule for edge cases.
