# Codex Galactica (pp. 75–89)

Ready-to-use pastiche factions provided by the book as inspiration/reskinning fodder, explicitly **not** affiliated with the IPs they riff on (Star Wars, Warhammer 40K, Star Trek, Doctor Who, Barsoom/John Carter). Each entry: Name (type — Leader/Specialist/Regular/Grunt/Galactic Hero/Villain/Alien Monster), Quality die, Traits, Weapons, Faction, and any Crew Trait / special rule.

## Federated Space (Star Trek-style)
- **The Captain** (Leader) d12 — Leader, Veteran, 9 Lives, Encouraging. Phazor.
- **Science Officer** (Above Average Regular) d10 — Smart, Defensive Fighter. Phazor.
- **Medical Officer** (Above Average Regular) d10 — Medic, Steady. Phazor.
- **Crewman** (Above Average Regular) d10 — Deadeye, Steady. Phazor.
- **Guys in Red Shirts** (Grunts) d8 — Nerves of Steel. Phazor.
- *Crew Trait*: **Above Average**.
- **The Phazor** (special weapon rule): 2 settings, chosen before firing. *Stun* — max result on the Wound Chart is Shaken, but always adds 2 Shock markers. *Kill* — weapon becomes Deadly (natural 10+ = auto Out of Action, no Wound roll). Using Kill to score an Out of Action costs the Crew **−1 Renown** for the scenario.
- **Teleporters** (special rule): action to remove your own model from the game entirely (can't return that game); before doing so roll d10 — on a 1 or 2 the teleporter fails to lock on and it can't be used this turn.

## Botz Collective (cybernetic hive mind)
- **Botz** (Regular) d10 — Soulless. Laser Pistol. *Crew Trait*: **Special Ops** (no Leader, 3 Regular spots).
- **Special rule — Capture**: an enemy Out of Action must stay on the table; if a Botz touches it, both teleport off-table into the Collective; Botz get 1 Renown per capture.

## Interdimensional Time Travelling Scientist (Doctor Who-style)
- **The Good Doctor** (Galactic Hero) d12 — Leader, Smart, Regeneration, 9 Lives ("some say more"). "The Wonderful Widget" (acts as Laser Pistol, +2 to any technology-based Task).
- **Companion** (Specialist) d10 — Dodge, Fearless. Any ranged pistol.
- Fill out with era-appropriate Regulars, e.g. **Earth Defense Force** (Regular) d10 — Nerves of Steel, Auto Rifle; *Crew Trait*: **Advanced Comms**.
- **Special rule — Undying**: the Good Doctor can't permanently die; never misses a campaign game due to being Out of Action (reborn instead).

## Roboton (robotic hive-mind conquerors)
- No Specialist; all Regulars get an extra Trait (i.e. treat as **Above Average**). All Soulless.
- **Roboton Leader** (Leader) d10 — Leader, Regenerate, Armored Hide, Smart. Laser Pistol.
- **Roboton** (Regular) d10 — Regenerate, Armored Hide. Laser Rifle.
- **Special rules**: *Capture* — as Botz, but they carry the downed enemy off rather than teleporting (1 Renown/capture). *Upgrade* — when wounded, roll d10; on 8+ they permanently adapt immunity to that damage type (does not work against the Good Doctor's Wonder Widget). *Special Weakness* — any gold-based weapon counts as Deadly against them.

## Using this Codex for other genres (author's closing notes)
The author explicitly encourages reskinning: Anime mecha/giant robots (use Grunt bases of 3 as regular infantry vs. "big guy" mech stat-lines built with normal Trait rules), Pulp/Victorian Sci-Fi/Steampunk (use Alien Monster rules for pulp creatures; classify pulp adventurers like Tarzan/the Phantom/John Carter as Galactic Heroes, rebranded "Heroes of the Age").
