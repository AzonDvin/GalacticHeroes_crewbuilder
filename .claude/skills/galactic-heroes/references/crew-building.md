# Building a Crew, Traits, Monsters/Heroes, and Psionics (pp. 24–35)

## Roster Structure

| Slot | Count | Traits | Equipment slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leader | 1 (required, unless Crew Trait says otherwise) | Leader Trait free + 3 chosen Traits | 5 |
| Specialist | 1 | 2 Traits | 4 (book calls this slot "Sergeant" once, elsewhere "Specialist" — same role, 4 slots) |
| Regular | 3 | 1 Trait each | 3 each |
| Grunts (replaces a Regular slot) | groups of 3 (4 with Tough as Nails) | 1 Trait shared by the whole group | 2 (shared by the group) |

Notes:
- No maximum Crew size, but the book warns >10 miniatures gets hard to track.
- A model may trade **2 Equipment slots for 1 Trait** during initial crew build.
- No Crew member may ever have more than **5 Traits** total.
- A model can take **one Negative Trait** to unlock one extra Positive Trait pick.
- After building the roster, pick **one Crew Trait** shared by the whole Crew.

### Grunts, in detail
- Use a **d8** Quality die, have only **1 Wound** (any Wound-Chart result above Shock puts them Out of Action).
- Come in a group of 3 (or 4 with the group's Tough as Nails trait), all sharing **one activation card** — but each Grunt takes its own actions and can go different places/do different things.
- They share the group's single Trait; a Special Card effect used on the activating card applies to the whole group (e.g., all get the reroll from a 7, all get the auto-recover from Queen of Spades).
- Individual conditions (Shock, Reload, etc.) are tracked per-Grunt, not shared.
- Normally only **one** Grunt group per Crew (Crew Trait "Horde" changes this — see below).
- Grunts cannot: be Ranged Fighters, use Heroic Sacrifice.

## Positive Traits (full list)

Traits marked **\*** are Leader-only.

- **Brawler** — once/turn, reroll your Close Combat action.
- **Camouflage** — extra −1 to be hit while in cover.
- **Concealed Weapon** — roll 2 dice, pick 1, for Close Combat (3 dice if combined with a "2" Special Card); once per game.
- **\*Cold Blooded** — +1 in Close Combat per Shock/Wound marker the *opponent* has.
- **Coolheaded** — +1 to all Shaken recovery rolls.
- **Cunning** — reroll any Task roll once/turn.
- **\*Damaging Aura** — action: place 3" burst template on self; anyone under it hit as Burst fire.
- **Deadeye** — +1 to Shoot rolls.
- **Deadly Strike** — reduces target's armor roll by −1 on a hit (stacks with weapon AP); pick Hand OR Ranged weapons for this.
- **Defensive Fighter** — if you're the Close Combat defender and win, immediately get a free attack back.
- **Deft** — reload costs 1 fewer action.
- **Determined** — ignore Shock/Wound penalties in Close Combat.
- **Dodge** — if shot at and missed, immediately move 5" toward cover.
- **Eagle Eyed** — weapon's short range +2", long range +4".
- **\*Encouraging** — action: remove all Shock from one Crew member within 12".
- **\*Energy Blast** — innate Shoot action, 6"/12" range, no Out of Ammo on a "1" — instead must rest a full turn to recharge before reuse.
- **Fancy Footwork** — win ties in Close Combat.
- **\*Fearless** — ignore multi-Shock-marker penalty on Recovery rolls (e.g., 3 Shock markers still only −1).
- **Fearsome Appearance** — enemies must pass a Regular (5+) Task to attack you in Close Combat, or must pick a different action.
- **Ferocious** — +1 to Close Combat Wound rolls.
- **Flurry of Blows** — if a Close Combat win only causes Shock, fight an immediate second round.
- **Flier** — jetpack/wings; ignores intervening terrain, can cover vertical height, assumed to land between moves.
- **Gadgeteer** — start each scenario with a free random Gear item.
- **Horseman** — mount gets an extra Trait.
- **\*Invulnerable** — reroll one failed Recovery roll, once/game.
- **Indomitable** — remove ALL Shock markers by spending 2 actions.
- **\*Impervious** — ignore Wound penalties on movement/attack/recovery.
- **\*Inspiring** — gain another Regular Crew member.
- **Infiltrator** — once scenario starting positions are set, immediately move 10" in any direction.
- **Killer** — +1 to Wound rolls when Shooting.
- **Large** — enemies +1 to hit you when Shooting; you get 4 Wounds (not 3) and +1 to your own Close Combat Wound rolls.
- **\*Leach** — cause a Wound/Out of Action in Close Combat → immediately heal 1 Wound.
- **\*Leader** — free for Leaders. +1 to any Recovery roll (Shaken or Wounded-and-Down) for a friendly model within 12" and line of sight. Natural "1" still routs/succumbs regardless.
- **Leaper** — +2" bonus when jumping (natural "1" still causes a fall).
- **Lightning Fast** — if successfully shot, immediately roll Hard Task (8+); success = the shot misses instead.
- **Loads of ammo** — ignore your first Out of Ammo result.
- **Loyal** — won't voluntarily leave the table while a Crew member remains; a "1" on a Shock Recovery roll doesn't remove you while teammates remain (Wound-recovery "1" can still take you Out of Action).
- **\*Lucky** — reroll any die roll once/turn.
- **Lunge** — Close Combat from 2" away (like a polearm).
- **\*Master of Melee** — roll 2 dice in Close Combat, keep best (3 dice combined with a "2" card).
- **Medic** — action: move adjacent to a Wounded model, Regular (5+) Task to heal 1 Wound; roll ≤1 inflicts a Wound by mistake instead.
- **Mule** — extra Equipment Slot.
- **Nerves of Steel** — ignore your first Shaken result of the game.
- **Night Vision** — see 24" at night instead of 12".
- **\*Pet** — attackers get −1 to hit you in Close Combat (unless they also have a pet); the pet can be sacrificed instead of you suffering a Wound/Out of Action. Pet isn't player-controlled (unlike the Animal trait).
- **\*Poison/Venom** — Close Combat Wound → target rolls again on the Wound Chart at end of every turn until healed by Queen of Hearts/Regeneration; rolling a natural "1" in Close Combat means you're out of poison for the rest of the game.
- **Pugilist** — no negative modifier for fighting unarmed.
- **Quick** — +1" per Move action.
- **Ranged Fighter** — d12 for Shooting (and Shoot Wound rolls), d8 for Close Combat (and its Wound rolls); all other rolls d10. Not available to Grunts.
- **Ranger** — ignore terrain movement penalties, +1 Close Combat while fighting in Rough terrain.
- **Running Attack** — after a Close Combat attack, use remaining movement to engage a new enemy.
- **Shape-Shifter** — maintain 2 roster profiles; action to switch, lasts rest of game.
- **Sharp Tongued** — pass an Easy (3+) Task to force an enemy in Close Combat to reroll their attack, once/turn.
- **Shieldmaster** — +1 on armor rolls; winning a Close Combat also lets you push the opponent 3" away.
- **\*Sixth Sense** — force an enemy to reroll one Shoot action against you, once/turn.
- **Slippery** — ignore the 1" Close Combat zone-of-control; can leave Close Combat on activation.
- **Smart** — +1 to any Task roll.
- **Sniper** — reroll a Shoot roll once/turn.
- **Solid as a Rock** — never pushed back or repositioned in Close Combat.
- **Soulless** — never take Shock results, but enemies get +1 to hit you when Shooting. (Robots/undead.)
- **Sprinter** — +3" free movement/turn, independent of other actions (e.g., aimed shot + still move 3").
- **Steady** — ignore Shock/Wound penalties when Shooting.
- **Stealthy** — −1 to be hit as a Shoot target; stacks with Creeping.
- **Strategist** — while on the field and not Wounded-and-down, Crew is dealt 1 extra card/turn; discard the least useful after seeing hands.
- **Street Fighter** — no negative modifier for facing multiple Close Combat opponents.
- **Strong** — +1 to knock down doors; can wield a 2-handed weapon one-handed (freeing a hand for a shield) or solo-crew a Team weapon.
- **Survivor** — +1 to campaign post-game survival roll.
- **Thundering Charge** — +1 to Close Combat roll if you're the attacker.
- **Tough as Nails** — 4 Wounds instead of 3 (doesn't stack with Large); Grunt groups with this trait have 4 members instead of 3.
- **Two Fisted** — roll a separate Close Combat die per opponent faced (still takes the outnumbered penalty unless offset); choose which die/weapon hits which opponent after seeing the rolls.
- **Two Gunned** — dual pistols, can Burst Fire; only goes Out of Ammo if 2+ Shoot rolls come up "1" in the same resolution.
- **Unstoppable** — once/turn, if you put an opponent Out of Action in Close Combat, immediately engage another enemy up to 5" away (or one already engaged).
- **Weapon Master** — +1 to Close Combat rolls when armed with a Close Combat weapon (stacks with the weapon's own bonus).
- **\*Veteran** — d12 for ALL rolls.
- **\*9 Lives** — once/game, convert an Out of Action result into 1 Wound instead.
- **Animal** — no shooting ability (may take an extra trait instead); can't make Task rolls; base move 6".
- **Amphibious** — no movement penalty in water, no Task roll needed for deep water.
- **Armored** — grants Light Armor equivalent, or bumps existing armor up one tier.
- **Bloodthirsty** — must charge any enemy within move range; +1 Close Combat roll and +1 Wound roll.
- **\*Bodyguard** — may redirect a Wound result to a friendly model within 2" instead of taking it yourself.
- **Brute** — no ranged combat; Close Combat die raised to d12 (d10 for Grunts); if you lose a Close Combat, opponent gets +1 on their Wound roll against you; max armor is Light Armor + Shield.
- **Small** — enemies −1 to hit you when Shooting; only 2 Wounds; −1 to your Close Combat Wound rolls.

A model can never take the same Trait twice.

## Negative Traits

Taking one Negative Trait unlocks one extra Positive Trait pick (max 1 Negative Trait/model). These are pure-downside effects with no offsetting benefit — unlike trade-off Traits such as Small, which stays in the main list because it pairs its drawback with a real benefit.

- **Brittle** — +1 to all Wound rolls made against this model.
- **Coward** — must pass a Regular (5+) Task before engaging in Close Combat.
- **Drunkard** — starts the game with 1 Wound already (all normal penalties apply); can heal normally via Queen of Hearts.
- **Green** — d8 for ALL rolls.
- **Lousy shot** — −1 to hit when Shooting.
- **Slow** — −1" to movement Actions.
- **Squeamish** — −1 to Wound rolls you make when Shooting.
- **Unlucky** — each enemy may reroll any die against you once/game.
- **Unskilled** — −1 to all Close Combat rolls.
- **Weakling** — only 2 Wounds instead of 3.

## Crew Traits (whole-Crew, pick 1)

- **Advanced Comms** — Crew dealt 1 extra card at the start of each round (discard the least favorite after seeing hands); requires ≥2 models still on the table.
- **Amphibious / Above Average** (no Specialist, but each Regular gets an extra Trait — Crew is then normally 4 models total; doesn't apply to Grunts) / **Armored** (whole Crew gets Armored Hide Trait or free Light Armor) / **Bloodthirsty** / **Camouflage** / **Cool Headed** / **Deadeye** — whole Crew gains that Trait, as applicable.
- **Distress Beacon** — pre-game roll d10; 5+ = joined by 2 Regulars or 1 Specialist.
- **Eagle Eyed / Fearless / Ferocious / Fliers / Infiltrators / Killers / Loyal / Quick / Shapeshifters / Soulless / Stealthy** — whole Crew gains that Trait.
- **Heroes All** — Crew is 4 Specialists, no Leader.
- **Horde** — Specialist slot AND all 3 Regular slots become Grunt groups instead.
- **Legendary** — start each game with 3 Renown points.
- **Ranger** — Crew ignores terrain penalties.
- **Special Ops** — no Leader; gain 3 Regular spots instead (i.e., 6 Regulars total in the base structure).
- **Stable** — Crew starts the game mounted.
- **Tactical** — replace one Regular with a Specialist.
- **Workshop** — start the game with 1 additional random Gear item.

A Crew member who already has that Trait individually doesn't get it twice from a matching Crew Trait.

## Alien Monsters & Galactic Heroes/Villains (p. 32–35)

Intended for special scenarios, ideally run by a GM/third party (or shared control between players).

**Alien Monsters**: get **5 Traits**, may use Leader-only Traits, plus these Monster-only Traits:
- **Cause Fear** — action, target within 12": both roll Quality dice; if monster rolls higher, subtract victim's roll and apply: 1–2 pulls back 1 move; 3–5 pulls back + Shock; 6–8 Helpless (paralyzed, must be rescued — teammate moves into contact + action to auto-recover next activation; while Helpless, rolls no Close Combat dice and enemies shooting it get +2); 9+ flees the table entirely. Modifiers: +2 if Fearless, −1 per existing Shock/Wound.
- **Extended Reach** — Close Combat range extended to 6".
- **Fire Breathing** — uses the large flame template.
- **Gaping Maw** — Out-of-Action kill in Close Combat = consumed; every friendly model within 12" takes a Shock marker.
- **God-like** — ignores Wound penalties on move/attack/recovery; "Out of Action" results are treated as Wounds instead (destroyed only after losing all Wounds).
- **Huge** — shooters get +2 to hit / −2 to Wound it; 6 Wounds; +2 to its own Close Combat Wound rolls; never pushed back.
- **Mesmerize** — like Cause Fear but: 1–3 fail (Shock only), 4–7 victim Helpless (trance), 8+ victim is enslaved and activates for the monster's side next turn (deal an extra card). Modifiers: +2 if Cunning, −1 per existing Shock/Wound.
- **Predatory** — Creep 5" instead of 3"; may start hidden, revealed only on activation.
- **Tunneler** — moves underground at normal speed, can't be attacked while underground, ignores terrain like a Flier.

**Galactic Heroes**: built exactly like a Leader (free Leader Trait + can pick any Trait including Leader-only ones). Bonus: activating on a **red suit** card (♥/♦) grants a **3rd action** that turn.

**Galactic Villains**: same construction, but the bonus action triggers off **black suit** cards (♠/♣) instead.

## Psionics (p. 34–35)

Optional add-on. A psychic follows either the **Path of Light** (Templars of Eternal Light) or **Path of Dark** (Templars of the Infinite Void); both count as Galactic Heroes/Villains and follow those rules (Light = bonus action on red suits, Dark = bonus action on black suits).

As an action, spend a psionic power from:

- **Braveheart** — Shock Recovery roll with +1.
- **Crush** *(Void/Dark only)* — mental Shoot attack, 6"/12" range, ignores cover.
- **Deflect** — if shot at from ≤12", make a Hard (8+) Task roll (Regular 5+ if shot from >12") to send the shot back at the shooter (roll to Wound using that weapon's normal bonuses).
- **Heal** *(Light only)* — action, in contact with another figure: remove 1 Wound or all Shock markers.
- **Leap** — add +d10" to any jump.
- **Push** — place 3" template up to 12" away; 5+ hits anyone under it → Shock marker + pushed 3" back; natural "1" hits the Templar with Shock instead.
- **Shield** — 3" template centered on self counts as Hard cover for everyone inside it until the Templar moves or attacks.
- **Telekinetic Grab** — "grab" an item up to 12" away: Hard (8+) Task for a 1"×1" item, +1 difficulty per additional inch of size (e.g. 3"×3" needs 10+). Moves 5"/action once grabbed; ends the activation on the ground (can't hold turn-to-turn). Natural "1" flings the item away d10" instead.
