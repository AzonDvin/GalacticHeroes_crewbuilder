# Equipment, Armor, Shields, Weapons, and Gear (pp. 36–48)

## Equipment slots by role
Leader 5 · Specialist 4 · Regular 3 · Grunt group 2 (shared, same loadout for the whole group). A model may trade 2 unused slots for 1 extra Trait at initial crew build.

## Armor

Three tiers. Roll d10 after a hit (before rolling to Wound) to see if armor absorbs it:

| Tier | Armor roll needed | Slots | Extra effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light Armor | 8+ | 1 | — |
| Heavy Armor | 6+ | 2 | −1" move/action, Task difficulty +1; grants the wearer the **Strong** Trait (power source) |
| Extra Heavy Armor | 5+ | 3 | −1" move/action, Task difficulty +2, **cannot Creep** |

**Resolving a hit with armor**: roll to Wound normally isn't skipped — instead, if the armor roll **succeeds**, reduce the Wound Chart result by one step (Out of Action → Wound → Shaken → No Effect). If it **fails**, resolve the Wound Chart result as normal. A natural **10+ on the attack roll** ("chink in the armor") skips the armor roll entirely — no save. **Each Wound already on the model reduces its Armor roll by −1** (armor degrades as the wearer is worn down). Naked/helm-only = effectively a 10+ armor roll (Grunts, with a d8, functionally can't benefit).

The **Armored** Trait bumps a model's own armor up one tier (max Extra Heavy).

### Shields
Take 1 Equipment slot. +1 to the Armor roll (stacks with worn armor; a bare model with a shield still gets an armor roll it wouldn't otherwise have). −1 to be shot at (doesn't stack with cover — it replaces it if cover would only be −1, i.e. you don't get −2 from a shield-in-light-cover). Removes the "outnumbered" penalty for the **2nd** attacker in Close Combat (3rd+ still applies −1). Can't Creep or use a 2-handed weapon while carrying one; can still use a spear/polearm one-handed but loses its polearm bonuses.
- **Buckler** (small round shield): keeps Creep ability, usable as a Two-Fisted weapon; loses the −1-to-be-shot bonus of a full shield.
- **Tower Shield/Pavise**: normal shield rules, plus an action to "plant" it for −2 to be shot (instead of −1); any model in base contact behind the bearer gets −1 to be shot too, until the bearer moves again.

## Weapons

### Hand weapons (Close Combat)
- **Dagger/Knife** — free, 0 slots. Wins ties vs. an unarmed opponent.
- **One-Handed** (1 slot each, usable with a shield): **Sword** +1 Close Combat roll · **Axe** +1 Wound roll · **Club/Mace/Flail** −1 Wound roll but adds an extra Shock marker regardless of the Wound Chart result · **Warhammer** −1 to opponent's Armor roll · **Lance** (mounted only) +1 Close Combat if mounted, +1 more if charging with it — but breaks (unusable again) if the wielder wins the fight · **Whip** — attacks from up to 6", opposed roll but a defending win means the attacker takes no harm at all; a Wound result on the whip's target = Helpless (needs a Recovery roll, or a teammate in contact + action, to escape).
- **Two-Handed** (2 slots each, no shield allowed): **2-Handed Sword** +1 Close Combat, AP1 · **2-Handed Axe** +1 Wound, AP1 · **2-Handed Warhammer** −2 to opponent's Armor roll.
- **Polearm-type** (2 slots, no shield): **Polearm** — 2" reach instead of 1", winner may hold opponent at range (and disengage freely next activation like Slippery) — loses this if it loses the fight; also a Defensive Weapon (+1 if charged). **Spear** — usable 1-handed with a shield (keeps the Defensive Weapon +1, loses the polearm reach/disengage) or 2-handed as a full polearm. **Pike** — as Polearm, but any enemy attacking a pike-armed model loses ALL their mounted/charge/lance bonuses.
- **Power Weapons**: add 1 Equipment slot to any of the above and it becomes **Deadly** (auto Out-of-Action on a natural Close Combat roll of 10+, no Wound roll needed) while keeping its normal weapon-type bonuses.

A model can dual-wield two One-Handed weapons, but must declare which one is striking before the roll (unless it has **Two-Fisted**, which lets it decide after seeing the roll — handy vs. multiple opponents).

### Primitive ranged weapons (require reload; see Weapon Chart for exact slots/ranges)
Bow, Crossbow, Warbow/Longbow (needs Strong), Sling (ignores the negative-1 penalty, reload 1), Throwing Knives/Axes/Javelins (no reload cost, but out of weapons on a roll of "1" or "2").

### Auto weapons
Auto Pistol, Submachine Gun (Burst-capable pistol range), Auto Rifle (Burst), Machine Gun (Team weapon, Burst even at long range, Ammo Hog).

### Energy weapons
- **Blasters** (Pistol/Rifle/Heavy) — all **Inaccurate** (−1 to hit, +1 to Wound).
- **Lasers** (Pistol/Rifle/Heavy) — all **Ammo Hogs**; AP1 (Pistol/Rifle) or AP2 (Heavy, also Burst + Team).
- **Mazers** — AP1, Ammo Hog, **ignore cover**.
- **Stunners** — ignore armor on human-sized-or-smaller targets; living targets roll Wound at −1, Soulless targets at +1.
- **Flamers** — teardrop template (thin end at shooter), Burst-weapon rules, short-range ring gives +1 to Wound; Limited Ammo; Specialist-only.

### Blast/heavy weapons
Grenades (Blast 3, thrown d10+3", natural "1" on the to-hit roll detonates in-hand at +1 Wound), Grenade Launcher (Blast 3, 10"/20" range), Rocket Launcher (Blast 5, Limited Ammo, Specialist-only), Shotgun (Splash), Sniper Rifle (Specialist-only), Heavy Blaster / Heavy Laser (Team unless wielder has Strong).

**Grenade sub-types**: Frag (natural 10+ to-hit gives everyone within 2" of the template a Shock marker too) · Goo/Webber (Hard 8+ Task or Helpless, +2 if Strong) · Shock (no Out-of-Action result possible, but adds a Shock marker; +1 Wound roll vs. Soulless) · Smoke (5" LOS-blocking template, dissipates on a d10 roll of 6+ at turn end) · Stun (2 Shock markers to anyone under the template; no effect on Soulless).

## Weapon Properties (glossary)

- **Ammo Hog** — Out of Ammo on a Shoot roll of 1 *or* 2 (also makes Burst fire easier to run dry — see below).
- **AP (Armor Piercing)** — reduces the target's armor tier by 1 (AP2 reduces by 2 levels). Light armor becomes none; Heavy drops to Light, etc.
- **Blast** — uses a Blast template (size given, e.g. "Blast 3"/"Blast 5"); center it on a target or point within range and line of sight; anyone at least half under the template is hit and rolled for individually. Both Blast templates have a +1-to-Wound inner ring. Miss → deviate: roll d10, the die's "point" gives direction, the number gives distance in inches (double the distance if the shot was at long range). Natural 10+ to-hit on a Blast weapon = dramatic: everyone within 2" of the template (even those not "under" it) takes a Shock marker. Natural "1": roll again — 6+ jams (add a Reload marker), 1–5 it detonates centered on the shooter instead.
- **Burst Fire** — usable at Short range (or Long for some heavy weapons); place the 3" Blast template (ignore its Wound-bonus ring), roll to hit everyone at least half-covered at −1 (inaccuracy of spraying). If the number rolled ≤ the number of targets under the template, the weapon goes Out of Ammo.
- **Deadly** — auto Out-of-Action on a natural attack roll of 10+, no Wound roll needed.
- **Inaccurate** — −1 to the Shoot roll, +1 to the Wound roll.
- **Limited Ammo** — an Out of Ammo result takes the weapon out for the rest of the game (no reloading).
- **Polearm** — 2" Close Combat reach instead of 1"; also a Defensive Weapon (+1 if charged); can't be combined with a shield.
- **Reload: N** — after firing, needs N consecutive actions to reload before firing again (e.g. Reload 1 = shoot with action 1, reload with action 2, ready next turn; Reload 2 = a full separate turn of both actions to reload).
- **Specialist** — only a Specialist or Leader may carry/use this weapon.
- **Splash** — anyone within 1" of the target (friend or foe) is also rolled to hit; at Long range this widens to 2", but splash targets take −1 to hit and −1 to Wound.
- **Team** — needs a Weapon Team to use (see below).

## Weapon Teams

Typically 2 miniatures (gunner + loader) moving/activating as one (dealt a single card). Moves at **4"/action** instead of normal rate. Needs **2 full, uninterrupted actions to set up** before it can fire the first time; a separate action to break it down again before moving. If shot at, randomly determine which team member is hit (unless targeted by Burst/Blast). If charged in Close Combat, the attacker fights both under the Multiple Combatants rule. If reduced to 1 member, the weapon can still fire but can't move — a new Crew member moving into contact re-forms the team (everyone is assumed cross-trained).
