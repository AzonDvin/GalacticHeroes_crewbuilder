# Scenarios, Campaign, and Post-Game Rules (pp. 51–58)

## Campaign & Renown

**Renown Points (RPs)** represent a Crew's galaxy-wide reputation. Earned by playing scenarios or via Traits; spent to replace/improve members, buy equipment, or as a reroll (1 RP = 1 reroll, once per turn, can't reroll a reroll). Unspent RPs carry over between games.

**Replacing a dead Crew member** costs: Specialist 5 RP, Regular 4 RP, Grunt 3 RP. (No listed cost for a Leader — see Promotion below.)

**Buying an extra Trait** for an existing member costs **double** the member's normal replacement cost: 8 RP for a Regular, 10 RP for a Specialist, 14 RP for a Leader. **No Crew member may exceed 5 Traits total.**

**Promotion** (alternative to replacing a dead member):
- Specialist → Leader: 4 Renown (grants the Leader Trait automatically).
- Regular → Specialist (only if the Specialist slot is vacant): 2 Renown.

If the Leader dies: either start a fresh Crew, or promote the Specialist as above.

No max Crew size, but >10 miniatures gets hard to track.

## Post-Game (survival check)

After a game, for each model that was Wounded or Out of Action, roll d10:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | "Just winged me" — full recovery, ready next game |
| 6–7 | "It's not so bad" — misses next game, ready the game after |
| 8 | "Call the Medic" — misses next game; returns already Wounded (−1 to all rolls/moves) until healed by Queen of Hearts |
| 9 | "Shot full of holes" — misses next game; returns permanently worse: must take a Negative Trait (or, if already has 2, must start as Wounded instead) |
| 10 | "It's the airlock for you" — dead, needs replacing |

Modifiers: **+1** if the model's side lost the scenario, **+1** if the model ended the game Out of Action (as opposed to just Wounded).

**Luck (optional rule)**: after checking survival for the whole Crew, the player may pick one model and treat any "It's the airlock for you" result on it as a survival instead (narrate it however — hit their lucky charm, flask, etc.). That model plays the next game as if nothing happened.

**Guns for Hire**: if a Crew starts a scenario outnumbered by 3+ models, add a group of pistol-armed Grunt mercenaries to even things up — they're one-scenario-only, not a permanent Crew addition.

**Useless Crews**: at any point in a campaign, a player may scrap their Crew and start a new one. Penalty: the new Crew may only start with the free Leader Trait — no other Traits — until Traits are bought back with Renown.

## Built-in Scenarios

### These Aren't the Bots You're Looking For
2–4 players, 3'×3'. A rebel Crew has one of two robots carrying half of a stolen set of plans; the other half is on a decoy robot loose in town — but which one? Rebels enter at A, must exit at B (a waiting ship); Imperial forces enter from C and D; extra decoy robots start at marked X spots.
- **Robot handling**: rebels assign an escort miniature (robot moves with it, stays within 1"), or leave it unescorted (moves randomly whenever a Joker triggers robot movement). Taking an unescorted robot costs a Close Combat-range contact + 1 action.
- **Joker mechanic**: shuffle a Joker into the deck (acts as a wild Ace of the lowest suit). When played: (a) every robot on the table gets a random d10 move (die "point" = direction, number = inches, stopping at obstacles/table edge), and (b) when a miniature spends an action in contact with a robot, roll d10 — a natural 10 reveals it's the one with the missing plans; otherwise +1 cumulative to the next robot checked, and the last unrevealed robot is automatically the one.
- **Victory**: Rebels get 5 RP for getting both robots to the ship (1 RP for just one); Imperial forces get 5 RP for recovering both intact (can't destroy them to win).
- Scales to 4+ players (bump table to 4'×4'); optional 6-turn soft time limit (roll d10 at end of turn 6: 6 or below ends it, 7+ extends by that roll's result as the new last turn).

### A Hive of Scum and Villainy
4 players, 3'×3'. Smugglers (enter A) sell stolen hyperfuel to pirates (start B); Imperial forces (enter C) and rebels (start D) try to intercept. Fuel starts on a hover-sledge/wagon/truck (max 6"/turn).
- **Sand Storm**: a Joker shuffled in triggers a storm on play (toggles on/off each time a Joker is drawn) — while active, LOS capped at 12" and all weapon ranges halved.
- **The Locals**: before shooting starts, buildings are free to enter; after the first shot fired, entering a *new* building draws an automatic close-range blaster shot from a defending local (resolved once, then that building is clear for the rest of the game).
- **Strafing Run**: at the end of any turn *without* an active sand storm, the Imperial player may place the 5" blast template anywhere, then roll d10 for a random drift (direction from the die's point, distance = the number) before resolving hits.
- **Victory**: Smugglers get 5 RP for completing the exchange (fuel in contact with a pirate/rebel + 2 actions); the buyer gets 5 RP for getting the fuel off their entry table-edge; Imperials get 5 RP for recovering the fuel. Fuel cannot be deliberately targeted to be destroyed (armored case).
- Scales to 4+ players (4'×4'); optional 6-turn time limit as above.

### Taking Down the Force Field
2–4 players, 2'×2'. Rebels must plant a Nova Bomb on an antenna array (X) to destroy the Doom Moon's shield generator before Imperial reinforcements (behind line A) overwhelm them; rebels enter within 6" of B.
- **The Bomb**: carried by a 2-Crew Weapon Team (hover suspensors, moves at Crew speed, half rate if reduced to 1 carrier); must be placed in contact with the antenna + 1 action to activate; detonates on the next King, Queen, or Jack drawn after activation.
- **The Natives**: shuffle in a Joker (both, if only 2 players); each time it's drawn, the rebel player gains a Grunt group of spear/bow-armed natives, entering at any table edge next turn, plus an extra card thereafter for each native group added.
- **Time limit**: roll d10 at end of turn 6 — 6 or below ends the scenario (fleet defeated), 7+ extends by that many additional turns.
- **Victory**: Rebels get 5 RP only for successfully detonating the bomb; Imperial forces get 5 RP for repelling the assault.
- Imperial forces in this scenario are all given the Loyal trait in addition to their normal Traits.

### Prepare to Repel Boarders!
2–4 players, 2'×3'. Boarding action aboard a crippled ship; attackers breach at A and must reach the Engine Room at Z before defenders (starting past the table's halfway line) stop them.
- **Teleporters**: two pads marked X — stepping onto one instantly moves the model to the other pad, carrying over any leftover movement from that action.
- **Zero Gs**: no gravity; models move only in straight lines (push off + drift) between activations.
- **Attackers** wear hardened Vacc-suits counting as Light Armor.
- **Time limit**: roll d10 at end of turn 6 — 6 or below ends it (engines engage, ship jumps), 7+ extends by that roll as the new last turn.
- **Victory**: 5 RP to whichever side achieves its goal (attackers capture the Engine Room / defenders prevent it in time).
- Scales to 4+ players (bump table size); give the disadvantaged side extra cover or troops if needed for balance.

All scenarios: if you lack the exact terrain/miniatures called for (robots, buildings, etc.), the book explicitly invites reskinning — e.g. substitute diplomats/technicians for robots, or a jungle planet with hidden fauna instead of a desert town.
